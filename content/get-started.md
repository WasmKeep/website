The tutorials are in the documentation:

1. [Your first actor](https://docs.wasmkeep.dev/tutorials/first-actor.html):
   a counter with its own database.
2. [Your first app](https://docs.wasmkeep.dev/tutorials/first-app.html):
   a front and its classes, run with `wasmkeep dev`.
3. [From one node to three](https://docs.wasmkeep.dev/tutorials/one-node-to-three.html):
   a cluster on one bucket.
4. [Going to production](https://docs.wasmkeep.dev/tutorials/going-to-production.html).

Releases, with their signatures, are on
[GitHub](https://github.com/WasmKeep/wasmkeep/releases). To check a
download, see
[installing the binary](https://docs.wasmkeep.dev/deploy/single-node.html).

This site is an example too: its
[source](https://github.com/WasmKeep/website) is a complete WasmKeep app.
