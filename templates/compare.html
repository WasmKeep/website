Pick them instead when

WasmKeep is young. These alternatives have different strengths; use the reviewed capabilities above and each project’s own docs to evaluate your application.

More on Durable Objects and celld below, with measurements.

  • Cloudflare Durable Objects

    You want a managed global network, years of production at Cloudflare's scale, a mature JavaScript ecosystem and no servers to run. Compare the costs for your workload on the Cost page.

  • celld

    You already have Workers code and want it on your own machines as it is, with the Workers platform pieces (KV, Queues, D1, R2) beside it. WasmKeep has its own API and no Workers compatibility.

  • Rivet

    Your backend is TypeScript and you want actors inside it, on Node.js or Bun.

  • Golem

    You want durable execution of a whole agent, replaying its interactions after a failure, with embedded SQLite available for structured per-agent state.

  • Orleans

    Your team writes .NET and your state belongs in a database you already run. Orleans is mature, and Microsoft runs it in its own services.

  • Dapr actors

    You run Kubernetes and want actors beside Dapr's other building blocks, from many languages, on a state store you already have.

  • Temporal

    Your problem is long-running workflows across services (orders, payments, provisioning) more than many small pieces of state.

  • Restate

    You want durable handlers and per-key state in front of services you already have, in your existing language, with one server to run.

In detail: Cloudflare Durable Objects

The closest programming model: an addressable object with its own storage, alarms and WebSockets. Do not assume identical scheduling: check the input/output gates and behavior across awaits in Cloudflare’s docs against WasmKeep’s whole-event serialization. Two things carry the name: workerd, the open-source runtime, and Cloudflare Workers, the hosted platform. The measurements below are historical local runtime comparisons, not measurements of Cloudflare’s managed network or of WasmKeep 0.4.2.

Pick Durable Objects for a managed global network, a mature JavaScript ecosystem and no servers to run; pick WasmKeep for durable actors on your servers, in a compiled language, with the data in your bucket. WasmKeep and Cloudflare Workers has the full comparison.

In detail: celld

In the measured celld v0.6.3 setup, the same idea was built on the Workers API: Durable Object-style actors on your own servers, each with its own SQLite database, replicated to a bucket. Both ran the same counter on the same machine and the same local bucket. These historical runs predate WasmKeep 0.4.2 and do not verify the latest celld release. celld v0.6.3, five runs of each, medians with the lowest and highest in brackets.
