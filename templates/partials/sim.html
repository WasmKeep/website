    Traffic & durability

    Illustrative traffic: two bodyless reads per write. Dots follow requests; amber waits, red gets 503. Read probes continue through a kill at zero load.

    Durable: acknowledged after upload. Plain: acknowledged locally and can be lost before upload. The demo stretches that window to make it visible; it is not a measured latency or loss rate.

    Nodes

    Up to five nodes here. Adding one does not move resident actors. Removal drains and uploads first, hands actors over, then stops the empty node.

    Actors 6 / 30

    Idle for 30 simulated seconds to hibernate (6 seconds here). Send read wakes it on the first node to claim it, with its saved database. A move is not a pin. Delete ends the actor and erases its history; the next request creates it empty.

    During failover, eligible bodyless GET/HEAD requests and WebSocket upgrades can wait (25 s default, with caps). Writes and the first requests that discover the failure get 503, Retry-After: 1. A 503 does not prove a write never ran. This demo uses safe read probes and does not retry writes. 0.4.2 request rules.
